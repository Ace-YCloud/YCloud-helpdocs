# Create/Import Contacts

## **Single Manual Import**

### Step 1

Log in to your [YCloud account](https://www.ycloud.com/console/#/entry/login), navigate to Contact > Contact list > + New contacts

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (36) (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Step 2

Click + New contacts > Add a new contact, fill in the contact information > Click Submit

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (37) (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (38) (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## **Bulk Manual Import**

### Step 1

Log in to your [YCloud account](https://www.ycloud.com/console/#/entry/login), navigate to Contact > Contact list > + New contacts

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (39) (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Step 2

Click + New contacts > Upload a file, upload the contact document; click “Next” to upload
